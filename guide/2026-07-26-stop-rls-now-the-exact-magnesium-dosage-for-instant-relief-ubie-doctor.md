Does magnesium help restless leg syndrome? Magnesium may help relieve symptoms of restless leg syndrome (RLS), but the ideal dosage has not been clearly established in current research.

Some small studies suggest that magnesium supplementation—particularly for people with a deficiency—may reduce leg discomfort, improve sleep quality, and ease the urge to move the legs at night. However, evidence remains limited, and results vary depending on the form of magnesium used (such as citrate, glycinate, or oxide) and individual health factors.

Because RLS symptoms can overlap with other conditions like iron deficiency, nerve issues, or sleep disorders, self-treating with supplements may delay finding the real cause.
