Can fever or illness trigger sleepwalking in children?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Fever and illness are known triggers of sleepwalking and sleep terrors in children, increasing the likelihood of these parasomnia episodes occurring during the night. When a child's body temperature rises or they feel unwell, disruptions to normal sleep cycles can prompt confused arousals from deep sleep, leading to walking, talking, or intense fear episodes they typically won't remember.

If your child is experiencing sleepwalking, night terrors, or unusual behaviors during sleep, it's important to identify whether an underlying illness, fever, or another factor may be responsible.
