What exercises can help alleviate ankle pain?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Ankle pain often improves with targeted muscle strengthening exercises for the foot and ankle. Common examples include calf raises, toe curls, ankle circles, and resistance band flexion. These exercises help stabilize the joint, reduce stiffness, and prevent future injury when performed consistently.

However, ankle pain can stem from many causes—sprains, tendonitis, arthritis, or even referred pain from the lower back—and the right treatment depends on the underlying issue.
