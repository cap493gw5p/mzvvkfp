What to Consider

Materials

Look for high-quality materials that ensure warmth, comfort, and longevity. By understanding what each fabric brings to the table, you can select the sweater that fits both your lifestyle and your wardrobe goals. Here’s a closer look at what you can expect from the most common options:

Cashmere

Cashmere is the gold standard for softness and luxury. It’s made from the undercoat of cashmere goats, resulting in an ultra-fine, buttery fabric. Expect it to feel lightweight yet warm, perfect for layering without adding bulk. Cashmere sweaters often have a higher price tag, but their timeless appeal and plush feel make them a worthwhile investment. Just remember: Cashmere requires delicate care, like handwashing or dry cleaning, to maintain its softness.

Merino Wool

Merino wool is a performance powerhouse. Derived from Merino sheep, this wool is fine, soft, and naturally moisture-wicking, making it ideal for regulating body temperature. Merino wool sweaters keep you warm without overheating and are often odor-resistant, so they’re great for active or travel-heavy lifestyles. Bonus: Many are machine washable, adding a dose of convenience.

Cotton

Cotton sweaters are breathable, lightweight, and perfect for transitional weather or layering indoors. They’re soft on the skin and great for those who find wool too itchy. Cotton doesn’t retain as much heat as wool, so it’s best suited for milder climates or casual settings. Expect easy care—most cotton sweaters are machine washable.

Wool Blends

Blended fabrics combine the best of both worlds. For example, wool-cashmere blends deliver the softness of cashmere with the durability of wool, while wool-cotton blends offer a balance of warmth and breathability. These blends are often more affordable and easier to care for than pure fibers.

Alpaca Wool

Alpaca wool is soft, silky, and hypoallergenic—great for those with sensitive skin. It’s warmer than regular wool but feels lighter, making it perfect for chilly days without feeling too heavy. Alpaca sweaters often have a natural luster, giving them a polished, luxurious look.

Fit

The best sweaters for men should strike the perfect balance—tailored enough to look polished, but comfortable enough for all-day wear. Pay attention to the shoulder and chest areas which should feel or fit roomy enough for an undershirt or a button-down shirt underneath, but also not too loose and still snug and slim enough for looking elevated during standalone wear. Sleeves should hit at the wrist bone when your arms are relaxed at your sides and the overall silhouette should be neither too tight nor too loose. A well-fitting sweater not only enhances your style but also maximizes comfort. And, of course, complements your body shape.

Versatility

Opt for designs that work across settings, whether it’s a classic crewneck for casual days, a half-zip for sporty outings, or a cable-knit for a dressier touch. A great sweater should transition with no problem from every location to the next.

How to Style Men's Sweaters

The key lies in understanding the different styles of sweaters and pairing them accordingly for various occasions. For a casual, laid-back look that you can wear every day, try a classic crewneck sweater with your go-to pants—perfect for running errands or grabbing coffee with friends. V-neck sweaters are ideal for a smart-casual or business-casual settings, whether you're at the office or at dinner, since they layer beautifully over collared shirts. (Zip sweaters also work as such, by the way.)

Cardigan sweaters are easy to dress up: layer a button-down shirt beneath a well-fitted cardigan for a sophisticated, professional-looking appearance, or pair them with khakis and tailored trousers to achieve a preppy look. Similarly, turtlenecks exude refinement and work for cocktail and business-casual attire, easily paired with tailored trousers and blazers for a polished, upscale look. For a more rugged, outdoorsy vibe, opt for a chunky cable knit or fisherman sweater.
