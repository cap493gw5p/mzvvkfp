How Shilajit Supports Cellular Repair

After an infection—viral or bacterial—cells undergo oxidative stress, mitochondrial dysfunction, and inflammation. Shilajit appears to counteract these challenges through multiple pathways:

Antioxidant activity

Fulvic acid scavenges free radicals, reducing oxidative damage to DNA, proteins, and lipids.

Humic substances modulate antioxidant enzymes (e.g., superoxide dismutase).

Increases ATP production by improving mitochondrial membrane potential.

Enhances cellular energy metabolism, critical for reversing fatigue.

Anti-inflammatory effects

Supports resolution of lingering inflammation in muscles and nerves.

Immunomodulation

May enhance macrophage and natural killer cell function.

Nutrient delivery

Fulvic acid chelates minerals, improving cellular uptake of zinc, iron, and magnesium—key cofactors in repair processes.

Scientific Evidence

Although more large-scale clinical trials are needed, existing studies are promising:

In vitro studies show fulvic acid strengthens mitochondrial function and protects neurons from oxidative damage, suggesting benefits for "brain fog" in post-viral syndromes.
