Get an accurate PCR test for travel to Los Angeles before your flight to avoid any potential delays.

Los Angeles Requirements

According to LAX, the airport will require anyone over sixteen entering the city from another country or city to complete a traveler form. This acknowledges the Center for Disease Control’s guidelines.

LAX does not require test results for domestic travelers. If you are travelling internationally, however, LAX will require proof of a negative test result 1-3 days before your flight, whether you are vaccinated or not.. They also recommend that travelers self-quarantine for 7-10 days after your flight to prevent any potential spread of infection.

LAX also has Covid vaccination and Covid testing sites. These sites have set hours, so ensure they fall within your traveling times with enough time to process results.
