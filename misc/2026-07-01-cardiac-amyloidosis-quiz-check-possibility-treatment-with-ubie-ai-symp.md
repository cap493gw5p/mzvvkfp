What is Cardiac Amyloidosis?

Cardiac amyloidosis is characterized by the accumulation of abnormal proteins in the heart tissues, affecting function.

Typical Symptoms of Cardiac Amyloidosis

Tongue swelling

Wheezing or whistling sounds when breathing

Waking up at night struggling to breathe

Difficulty breathing

Breathing is harder when lying down, improves when sitting up

Swelling of the arms or hands

Producing phlegm that contains blood

Heart pounding

Diagnostic Questions for Cardiac Amyloidosis

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Is your tongue swollen?

Do you experience wheezing or whistling sounds when breathing?

Treatment of Cardiac Amyloidosis

Cardiac amyloidosis treatment focuses on managing symptoms and preventing further damage. Options include diuretics for fluid retention, medications to manage heart failure, and chemotherapy or targeted therapies to address the underlying amyloidosis.
