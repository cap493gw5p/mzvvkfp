Tactile hallucinations are sensations that feel real but are not caused by actual stimuli. These can sometimes be caused by medications. If a medication is suspected, consulting a healthcare provider about adjusting or changing the medication might help.

Another potential cause of tactile hallucinations is a vitamin B12 deficiency, as highlighted by Sahu, Thippeswamy, and Chaturvedi. Ensuring adequate intake of vitamin B12 through diet or supplements can help manage symptoms.

For those experiencing tactile hallucinations, it is important to talk to a healthcare professional to identify and address the specific cause.
