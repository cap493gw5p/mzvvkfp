What Are Peptides and How Do They Work?

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that act as signaling molecules in the body. They influence various physiological functions by binding to specific receptors, triggering cellular responses. (1) Some peptides mimic hormones, while others affect neurotransmitters or immune system activity.

When it comes to weight loss and metabolism, certain peptides, such as GLP-1 receptor agonists can stimulate the release of growth hormone, enhance energy production, and even regulate appetite and fat breakdown. (2) These compounds target underlying biological processes such as appetite regulation, glucose metabolism, and, in some cases, fat breakdown.

Because peptides work within precise cellular or hormonal pathways, they should always be administered under medical supervision. At Gameday Men’s Health, every peptide therapy program begins with comprehensive lab work to ensure treatments are safe, effective, and tailored to each individual.
