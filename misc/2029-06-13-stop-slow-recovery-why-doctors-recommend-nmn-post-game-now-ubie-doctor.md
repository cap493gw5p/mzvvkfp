5. Practical Considerations for NMN Use

Before adding NMN to your regimen, keep these points in mind:

Dosage: Typical doses in human studies range from 250–500 mg/day.

Timing: Take NMN in the morning or pre-workout to align with your body's circadian rhythm.

Formulation: Choose a reputable brand that uses pure NMN (avoiding nicotinamide or other precursors).

Safety: Short-term studies report minimal side effects (mild nausea or digestive upset). Long-term safety data are still forthcoming.

Interactions: If you take medications or have chronic conditions, discuss NMN with your physician to rule out potential interactions.

6. Complementary Strategies for Faster Recovery

NMN isn't a silver bullet. For optimal results, pair supplementation with proven recovery practices:

Quality sleep: Aim for 7–9 hours of uninterrupted rest to support cellular repair.

Balanced nutrition: Prioritize lean protein, healthy fats, antioxidants (berries, leafy greens), and complex carbs.

Hydration: Maintain fluid and electrolyte balance before, during, and after exercise.

Active recovery: Incorporate light movement (walking, yoga) to promote blood flow and metabolite clearance.

Cold therapies: Icing or cold-water immersion can reduce inflammation when used appropriately.

By addressing all aspects of recovery, you'll maximize the benefits of NMN and shorten your athletic recovery time even further.
