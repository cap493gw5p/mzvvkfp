# Everything You Need to Know About the Final Reebok x Pyer Moss Collection

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

It's been five years since Kerby Jean-Raymond—the creative director of Pyer Moss—began collaborating with Reebok, creating a stream of highly anticipated sneakers, activewear, and more. And now, the time has come for something new. But before the supremely popular pairing part ways, they're dropping its fourth and final collection—the first drop will be available to shop tomorrow, March 26.

Earlier this month, Jean-Raymond announced he would be stepping down from his role as Reebok’s global creative director, a position he assumed in 2020. With the announcement came the news of Reebok by Pyer Moss Collection 4, the designer’s parting mark on the activewear brand.

Read More: Best Activewear Brands for Men

This last collection—which ranges from $80 to $800—features activewear-inspired apparel and outerwear like hoodies, windbreakers, and a puffer jacket. Each piece centers on the idea of spirituality and the ability to connect with others within your community. While Reebok and Pyer Moss have launched sneakers together in the past, Jean-Raymond shared in an Instagram post that the sneakers featured in the campaign, the "Experiment G," did not make it into production. However, if you're keen on snagging a pair, the designer also mentioned that there will be about 20 samples that they "may let go of" when the first part of the collection drops.
