What Is a PRP Facial?

A PRP facial uses your own blood to harness growth factors and platelets that stimulate collagen and elastin production.Steps involved:

Draw a small sample of your blood (usually 10–20 mL).

Spin it in a centrifuge to separate plasma rich in platelets.

Apply or inject this platelet-rich plasma into the face via microneedling or direct injections.

The growth factors in PRP encourage tissue repair, cell turnover, and increased circulation.

Why Choose PRP for Face Before and After Transformations?

People opt for PRP facials to address:

Fine lines and wrinkles

Sun damage and uneven tone

Acne scars and enlarged pores

Loss of skin elasticity

Dull complexion

Unlike synthetic fillers, PRP uses your body's own healing abilities, minimizing the risk of allergic reactions.

Realistic Expectations: Before and After

Immediate vs. Long-Term Results

Immediately post-treatment: Mild redness, pinpoint bleeding (if microneedling), and a sensation similar to a mild sunburn.

First week: Skin may feel tighter, slightly plumper, and exhibit a healthy glow. Slight peeling or flaking can occur as old cells shed.

4–6 weeks: Noticeable improvement in texture, reduced fine lines, and evening of skin tone as collagen rebuilds.

3 months and beyond: Optimal collagen remodeling leads to firmer, more youthful skin. Some patients report continued improvement up to six months.

Potential Risks and Side Effects

While complications are rare, know the possibilities:

Bruising, bleeding, or pinpoint scabs at injection sites

Temporary swelling or redness

Risk of infection (minimized by sterile technique)

Rarely, allergic reaction to topical numbing agents

Maintenance and Follow-Up

Initial protocol: 3 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart

Maintenance: 1–2 sessions per year

Consistent sun protection (SPF 30+), antioxidant serums, and gentle exfoliation support and prolong results.

Preparing for Your First PRP Facial

Discontinue blood-thinning supplements (e.g., fish oil, vitamin E) one week prior, if approved by your doctor

Stay hydrated and eat a balanced meal before your appointment

Arrive with clean skin, free from heavy makeup or skincare products

Aftercare Tips

Keep skin hydrated with a gentle moisturizer

Avoid direct sun; use broad-spectrum SPF daily

Skip intense workouts, saunas, and hot tubs for 48 hours

Use a gentle cleanser; avoid exfoliants until skin fully recovers

When to Seek Medical Advice

PRP facials are generally safe, but if you experience any unusual symptoms after treatment, it's important to get them checked.
