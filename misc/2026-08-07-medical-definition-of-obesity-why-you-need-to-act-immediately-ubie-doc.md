Internal Medicine

Obesity is a chronic medical condition characterized by excessive body fat accumulation that increases the risk of serious health problems, including heart disease, type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, stroke, and certain cancers. It is typically diagnosed when a person's body mass index (BMI) is 30 or higher, though factors like waist circumference and overall health are also considered. Obesity results from a complex mix of genetic, behavioral, environmental, and metabolic factors—not simply overeating or lack of willpower.
