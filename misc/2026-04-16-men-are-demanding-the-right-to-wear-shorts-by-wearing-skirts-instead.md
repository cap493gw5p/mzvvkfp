But some dress codes prohibit shorts in the workplace—and men across Europe are protesting.

[Shorts By Wearing Skirts](https://antimicrobial-hub.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/6525573315.html)

Bus drivers of the CFDT Semitan union in Nantes, France, are showing up to work in skirts since they’re not allowed to wear shorts on the job, despite the fact that it’s hot as hell out there.

The drivers tell the International Business Times that the dress code rule against shorts is “absurd,” especially since the area is currently in the midst of a heat wave. They point out that female drivers are allowed to wear skirts, so…

Plus, one driver told Franceinfo that travelers wouldn’t even see their calves since they’re seated when they drive. It’s not as simple as cranking up the AC: Union member Gabriel Magner told The Telegraph that high temperatures behind the dashboard combined with the fact that the buses don’t have air conditioning makes it “unbearable” to work in pants. He said that the "modern approach" to the problem would be to allow drivers to wear long shorts. He also said that the fact that women can wear skirts and men can’t wear shorts is “discrimination.”
