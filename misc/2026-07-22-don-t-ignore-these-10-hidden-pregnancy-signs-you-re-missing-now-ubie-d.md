Explanation

Some early signals of pregnancy can be very gentle and easily overlooked. For instance, you might feel a little bit nauseous in the morning or feel extra tired during the day. These mild symptoms can blend in with the usual daily feelings. Your breasts might feel a bit more tender or swollen, even if you don't see much change in size. Some people also experience small changes in urination habits; this might mean feeling like you need to go more often or, in rare cases, even having a bit of trouble with it. Sometimes, there might be a little discomfort or bloating in your tummy as your body starts to adjust for the new baby. These signs are very soft and can be easy to dismiss or confuse with other health issues or normal body changes, especially during a busy or stressful time. Paying closer attention to these small changes over time can help you notice that they might be linked to pregnancy.
