PEDRO PASCAL’S LOOK at the Cannes Film Festival certainly turned some heads over the weekend. It’s not often you see an A-list actor, in the prime of his career, and during what is perhaps his most iconic role ever, casually sporting a form-fitting black muscle tee, guns exposed, at one of the biggest film festivals in the world. Let’s just say the look went over quite swimmingly with the fans, the press, and the whole waking public.

Pedro wore the simple yet statement-making outfit (muscle tees, in spring? groundbreaking) for the Eddington photocall at Cannes, and the minimalist look, from, quite fittingly, Calvin Klein’s Fall 2025 collection, was a steep departure from some of the eccentric red carpet styles we’ve seen in recent memory. And, naturally, we’re no strangers to the classic muscle tee here at Men’s Health; we welcome a good excuse to wear one, especially if it looks like it did on Mr. Pascal. He’s cracked the code on wearing a top, historically reserved for the gym or weekend loafing sessions, in a large public setting and made it look like it actually belonged on the red carpet—and, more so, that we were fools all this time for thinking otherwise. A feat that is, by no means, as simple as putting on a cutoff shirt and calling it a day.
