# Aesthetic Nurse Practitioner - LIVV

About the Role:

The Aesthetic Nurse Practitioner’s (NP) role is to provide appropriate and safe nursing care to patients while overseeing and providing education and support to other Aesthetic RNs. The NP shall ensure the implementation of safe practice standards, continuity of individual patient care, and maintain a well-controlled treatment environment. The NP shall assess for skin aging symptoms or other skin-related health concerns and complete good faith medical exams in accordance with practice standards, administer medical aesthetic services and procedures and perform pre and post-treatment care, as required. The NP shall complete research to stay up-to-date on new procedures and protocols, through continued education as needed by licensure. The NP shall also consult with, screen, and schedule patients.

What You’ll Do:

Provide clinical and professional medical services to patients within the scope of nurse practitioners qualifications and consistent with accepted standards of medical practice and consistent with the reasonable productivity standards adopted by the corporation and the nurse practitioner (KPIs).

Completes GFME, follow-up consultations and provides proper evaluation of patient’s history, aesthetic goals, diagnosis, and assesses previous medical history.

Creates personalized aesthetic plan for patients.

Prescribes medication when needed

Provide general patient care at the site specified by performance of accepted procedures and commonly used therapies and provisions of appropriate support services.
