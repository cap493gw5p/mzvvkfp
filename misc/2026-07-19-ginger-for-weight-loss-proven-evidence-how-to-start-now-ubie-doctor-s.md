Published on: 7/14/2026

What is the evidence supporting ginger's role in weight loss, and how should it be consumed?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Ginger may support weight loss by boosting metabolism, increasing calorie burn, reducing appetite, and improving fat absorption. Research suggests ginger can help lower body weight, waist-to-hip ratio, and fasting blood sugar levels. It can be consumed as fresh root, tea, powder, or supplements as part of a balanced diet.

However, unexplained weight changes, persistent fatigue, or difficulty losing weight despite lifestyle efforts can sometimes signal an underlying health issue that ginger alone won't fix. Ginger has shown potential benefits for weight loss, primarily by increasing the body's ability to burn calories and enhancing metabolism.

[Doctor's Note](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/)

Increased Calorie Burning: One study found that ginger consumption can enhance the thermic effect of food, which means it helps the body burn more calories when digesting food.

Metabolic Improvements: Another study indicated that ginger might improve certain metabolic parameters, which can contribute to weight loss.

Clinical Trials: A clinical trial showed promising results for ginger's role in weight management, suggesting that it could be beneficial when included in a healthy diet.
