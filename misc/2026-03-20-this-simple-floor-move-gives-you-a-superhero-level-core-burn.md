# This Simple Floor Move Gives You a Superhero-Level Core Burn

For dedicated exercisers, building up a super hero-esque physique might be all about ascending to new heights. To get there, though, sometimes it pays to stay low to the ground.

You can use simple, floor-level exercises like the bear crawl to activate your core muscles and hone your body control and spinal stability, which can help to make tougher multi-joint movements easier. Trainer Don Saladino uses bear crawls to challenge his superhero actor clients like Ryan Reynolds and Sebastian Stan to concentrate on the small, subtle movements that help to make every part of a workout better.

[Superhero-Level Core](https://innate-signal.jcesqc8.com/blog/1785347100.html)

To perform the bear crawl, all you need is some space on the gym floor. Just take a spot on the ground and get ready to tap into your primal instincts to get moving.

Find a spot on the floor where your have 10 yards or more of empty space in front of you.

Get into an all-fours position with your weight on your palms and toes. Your knees should not be touching the ground.

Keep your back flat. Squeeze your core to maintain that neutral spine position.

[You Superhero-Level Core](https://jointrevive.xianfengchem.com/)

[You Superhero-Level Core](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/blog/3336604573.html)

To move, lift your right hand and left foot forward at the same time to go forward. On your next step, move your left hand and right foot simultaneously. Keep your core engaged and keep your gaze down and slightly in front of you.

[Move Gives You](https://dermaloom.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/9807610774.html)

If you want to make your bear crawl more effective, add a load. One of the best options is a chain, which can challenge you by putting more weight on your back and behind you, as you drag any additional links behind you as you go.

To start, crawl backwards and forwards for 10 yards at a time. Use bear crawls as a warmup mobility exercise before a workout or as a core move in your ab circuit. For more superhero-body building moves, you can check out all of our Superhero Fit Workouts or Saladino's full program.
