Leg pain involving both muscles and nerves can stem from several underlying causes. Common culprits include overuse injuries and muscle strain—frequently seen in athletes—as well as nerve-related conditions like peripheral neuropathy, which may result from peripheral arterial disease (PAD), diabetes, or nerve compression. Identifying the root cause is essential, since muscle-related and nerve-related pain often require very different treatment approaches.

Because leg pain can range from a minor strain to a sign of a more serious vascular or neurological condition, it's important not to guess.
