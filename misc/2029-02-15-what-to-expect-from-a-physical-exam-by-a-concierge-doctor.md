What is a Physical Exam?

As its name implies, a physical exam is when a medical professional examines your overall physical well-being and health. These exams should be a part of every adult’s wellness checklist, and it’s a good idea to receive a physical once a year or every two years.

Because physicals are a form of preventive medicine, you don’t have to be sick to schedule one. Many diseases and conditions can start forming in your body before you develop physical symptoms. In some cases, it can be difficult to treat your condition by the time you feel physically ill. By getting a physical exam, your doctor can catch these illnesses and begin treatment pre-emptively.

Different Types of Physical Exams

What happens during your physical exam depends on your examination type. The different types of physicals vary depending on your reason for getting the examination. For instance, employment and pre-employment physical exams are different than school or sports physicals.

Annual physical exam – is a general wellness check that you can schedule on your own with a healthcare provider.

Sports physicals – if you or your child is looking to join a sports team for school or professional purposes, you may need a sports physical. Sports physicals are similar to annual physicals but much more in-depth and are performed to determine if you can perform the activities required by your sport.

School physicals – depending on the type of school you or your children are planning to attend, you may need to receive a school physical. Physicals for school are not invasive and are designed to screen for health problems and ensure the student meets health requirements as determined by the school or team.

DOT physical exam – DOT physicals are physical examinations specifically for those seeking jobs involving transportation. These are typically required for truckers, bus drivers, and anyone operating heavy machinery.

Pre-employment physicals – a pre-employment physical exam is one of the most common types of physicals. They are performed to determine whether you can perform the duties for which you will be responsible.

Where to Get a Physical Exam?

You can get a physical exam from various healthcare providers and professionals. Anyone from your primary care physician (PCP) to an urgent care clinic can administer a physical exam. The advantage of going to your PCP is that, as your family doctor, you may already have a relationship with them.

Why might I Need a Physical Exam?

There are many different reasons why you might need a physical exam. It is a good idea to get a physical exam every three years if you’re under 50, but you should receive an annual exam once you hit 50. You should also see your doctor more frequently if you have or are concerned about developing chronic conditions.

Additionally, many jobs and employers require more frequent physical exams, such as DOT and medical personnel. Depending on the type of exam you are receiving and who requires it, there might also be additional paperwork and procedures. Be sure to check with the doctor performing your exam to ensure you bring everything you need to your appointment.

What is the Cost of a Physical Exam?

Physical exam costs vary from person to person and doctor to doctor. Your insurance will also play a role in the cost of a physical exam because some insurance plans are more comprehensive than others. One of the biggest things that will drive up the cost of a wellness check is whether or not you get it from your primary care physician or if you go to an urgent care center.

While urgent care centers are great for emergencies and certain situations, they charge a hefty sum for their work. Therefore, you may end up paying much more for an urgent care physical exam than at your PCP or with at-home physical exams.
