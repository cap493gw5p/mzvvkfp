When to Seek Medical Help

Hangovers generally do not last more than 24 hours, and the effects of alcohol tend to wear off over time, so if you notice your symptoms not subsiding, this is a sign that something may be wrong.

You should also be on the lookout for severe symptoms such as blood in vomit or stool, painful or prolonged vertigo, loss of consciousness, and any irregularities with the heart. As someone already at risk for heart problems, it is better to be cautious and seek medical treatment if you feel anything out of the ordinary.
