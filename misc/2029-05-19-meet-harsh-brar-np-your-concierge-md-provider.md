Full Bio

Harsh Brar, MSN, FNP-C, is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner with expertise in pain medicine and management, addiction medicine, concierge care, and holistic health. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Nursing from West Coast University and his Master of Science in Nursing from Samuel Merritt University. His training and clinical experience allow him to approach each patient’s concerns with a broad understanding of both conventional treatment options and whole-person wellness. Harsh takes a compassionate, patient-centered approach to care, beginning with careful listening and a clear understanding of each patient’s symptoms, priorities, and goals. He works to create individualized treatment plans that address immediate concerns while also supporting long-term health, function, and quality of life. His interests in pain care, addiction medicine, and holistic health shape a thoughtful approach that considers the physical, emotional, and lifestyle factors affecting each patient. Harsh is committed to ongoing education and clinical excellence so he can continue providing comprehensive, evidence-based care as treatment options and patient needs evolve.
