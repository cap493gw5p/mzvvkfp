# Vomiting and Diarrhea in Pregnancy: Causes & When to Act Now | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/14/2026

What can cause vomiting and diarrhea during pregnancy?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Vomiting and diarrhea during pregnancy are common and can result from several causes, including viral or bacterial infections, food poisoning, hormonal shifts, or pregnancy-specific conditions such as hyperemesis gravidarum. While mild cases often resolve on their own, persistent or severe symptoms can lead to dehydration, electrolyte imbalances, and nutritional concerns that may affect both mother and baby. Contact your healthcare provider promptly if symptoms worsen, if you can't keep fluids down, or if you notice signs of dehydration like dark urine, dizziness, or reduced urination.

Because pregnancy symptoms can overlap with many conditions, identifying the likely cause early helps you take the right next step—whether that's home care, a call to your OB, or an urgent visit. One common cause is a stomach bug or food poisoning from bacteria or a virus. In some cases, severe nausea and vomiting, known as hyperemesis gravidarum, can happen. This is more than just morning sickness, and it may also be linked with diarrhea and dehydration.

Hormonal changes during pregnancy can also make your stomach more sensitive and upset. It is important to rest, drink plenty of fluids, and try to eat small, bland meals if you feel up to it.

However, if you start to feel weak, dizzy, or if you are not peeing much, these could be signs of dehydration. In these situations, or if your symptoms continue for a long time, you should call your doctor or healthcare provider right away. They can check you and your baby, give you the right advice, and help you feel better.
