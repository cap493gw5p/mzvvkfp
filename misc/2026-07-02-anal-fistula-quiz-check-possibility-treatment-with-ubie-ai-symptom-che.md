What is Anal Fistula?

This condition involves a tunnel forming between the anus and the surrounding skin. Patients are at risk for recurrent infections, often connected to inflammatory bowel diseases like Crohn's disease.

Typical Symptoms of Anal Fistula

Diagnosed with Crohn's disease in the past

Hemorrhoids or lumps around the anus

Itching around the anus

Pus from around the anus or anus itself

Diagnostic Questions for Anal Fistula

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Have you been diagnosed with Crohn's disease?

Do you have hemorrhoids or lumps around your anus?

Do you have anal itching?

Do you have pus around or in the anus?

Are you experiencing anal pain?

Treatment of Anal Fistula

Surgery is typically needed since they rarely heal independently. Treating the underlying cause, such as bowel inflammation, helps prevent fistulas from coming back.
