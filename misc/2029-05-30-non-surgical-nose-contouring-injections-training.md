About this lesson

Enhance your skills in achieving a beautifully sculpted nose with non-surgical rhinoplasty using the Belotero range of products. This comprehensive advanced training video guides medical professionals through the process of assessing and improving nasal features, from evaluating nose shape to applying precise dermal fillers to enhance structure and contour. Learn how to address concerns such as flatness, wide nostrils, and an acute nose-to-forehead angle with expert techniques. Step-by-step instruction demonstrates the application of the filler to create support along the dorsum and smooth the profile for a more refined look. With a focus on comfort and minimal pain, this video provides an effective, non-invasive solution to nasal reshaping, delivering natural-looking, symmetrical results. Equip yourself with the knowledge and techniques to transform your patient’s appearance with confidence and achieve beautiful, balanced nasal contours.
