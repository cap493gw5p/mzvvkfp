How long does it take to see results from PRP?

Most patients begin noticing improvements in skin texture, elasticity, or hair growth within a few weeks. Full results typically develop over 3 to 6 months as collagen production and tissue regeneration continue.

Is PRP treatment painful?

PRP injections cause minimal discomfort, similar to a light pinch or mild pressure. A topical numbing cream is applied before treatment to enhance comfort, making the procedure well-tolerated by most patients.

How long do PRP results last?

Results vary by individual, but skin rejuvenation benefits can last 6 to 12 months, while hair restoration treatments may require maintenance sessions every 4 to 6 months for sustained growth.
