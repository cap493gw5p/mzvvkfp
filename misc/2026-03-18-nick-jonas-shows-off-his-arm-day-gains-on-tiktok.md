# Nick Jonas Shows Off His Arm Day Gains on TikTok

[Gains on TikTok](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/)

Nick Jonas is doing What A Man Gotta Do to grow his arms. In a new TikTok video (which he also shared to his Instagram page), the singer and actor can be seen Burnin' Up his biceps, performing a set of bicep curls using a pair of adjustable dumbbells.

The post elicited, perhaps somewhat predictably, an influx of comments, with many fans of the former teen heartthrob expressing their thirst—among them his own wife, actress Priyanka Chopra Jonas, who made her feelings clear via a drooling smiley face and heart eyes emoji—and others offering critiques of Jonas' weightlifting technique.

Looking to step up your bicep curl game ? It's not just about adding weight. As Men's Health fitness director Ebenezer Samuel C.S.C.S. explains in a recent bicep curls "Level Up" tutorial video, the secret to unlocking big arms isn't just how much you lift, but how you lift.

Jonas' workout post also garnered significantly more attention than his previous Instagram post, a preview of his upcoming Super Bowl commercial. In the ad, Jonas extolls the benefits of Dexcom, a connected health system which enables people living with diabetes to monitor and manage their blood glucose levels.

"Drones deliver packages, and people with diabetes are still pricking their fingers? What?" Says Jonas, who was diagnosed with Type 1 diabetes at the age of 13, in the ad, which showcases the many advances humanity has made in technology—including CGI effects which turn the 28-year-old star into an old man.
