TIMEX WATCHES for men have always been known for affordability. Over the past decade, the American watchmaker has become a destination for stylish timekeeping as well. Remarkably, this was done at an affordable price point, with most watches still selling in the $100 to $300 rage. As if that weren’t enough, you can add "impressive variety" to Timex’s list of accolades. A quick browse through the watch brand’s website has everything from sport watches to waterproof watches and a boatload of chronographs. There really is something for everybody and every occasion.

Collaborations with tried-and-true menswear brands like J.Crew, Todd Snyder, and Brooks Brothers helped put Timex on the map years ago. These partnerships turned out to just be a hint at what the brand had to offer. Timex has done an excellent job mining a rich heritage of classic styles, while offering an accessible alternative to the vintage watches we love but often can’t get our hands on, much less afford. With every watch category, movement, size, and color available—all for under $300—we figured that we should narrow down your search a bit. So whether you're shopping for a go-to watch on a budget or looking to add some variety to your collection, here are the 25 best Timex watches for men.

Timex Easy Reader® 38mm Stainless Steel Expansion Band

If there is a "signature Timex," it is the Easy Reader, which tells you everything you need to know about the brand. A simpler, more straightforward watch face couldn't be designed. Yet it is remarkably pleasant to look at and, of course, easy to read. A date window, Indiglo backlight, and 30 M water resistance add discrete layers of functionality. Our favorite feature, however, is the steel expansion band because you just don't see that anymore.

Timex M79 Automatic 40mm Stainless Steel Bracelet Watch

The newest addition to Timex's M79 line exemplifies everything we've come to love about the brand in recent years. Features that made the M79 so popular, like the perfectly sized 40 mm body with angular edges, the "T" hour hand, and day-date window are all there. Now you've got an updated steel bracelet, green and black rotating bezel, and a charcoal gray face with a subtle sunburst texture. Underneath all that is a dependable automatic movement.

Timex Expedition North Sierra 40mm Fabric Strap Watch

Adventure watches should be tough, water resistant, easy to read, comfortable, and a little flashy. What they don't have to be is expensive. Look no further than the Expedition North Sierra for a field watch that provides everything you need for under $100. While a few different color and strap varieties are available, we recommend the navy and orange nato strap because it is comfortable, breathable, and waterproof.

Timex Marlin® Automatic 40mm Leather Strap Watch

Men's watches were sleek and understated in the 1960s, which is when the Marlin line first came out. Timex upgraded the guts, both in size and function, with a modern automatic movement. While staying true to the retro face and case designs that sets this watch apart. The dark brown leather bands emphasizes the slight shimmer of the navy blue face. This is a dress watch through and through.

Timex Marlin® Hand-Wound California Dial 34mm Leather Strap Watch

Known as a "California dial," the combination of roman numerals on the top half and Arabic numerals on the bottom half is an ultra rare commodity in the vintage watch market. Thankfully, Timex made it easy to find one with this compact 34 mm Marlin. The mid-century vintage look carries on through the green faux gator leather strap. What's most fun about this watch is the hand-wound movement, which adds a little tactile connection to your timepiece.

Timex Standard Sub-Second 40mm Apple Skin Leather Strap Watch

Placing the second hand on a smaller inset dial was common in the early twentieth century. Timex modernized this style with a faux leather band made from recycled apple skin. Balancing vintage design aspects with 2023 aesthetics has become a key characteristic of the brand, and this watch is the perfect example.

Timex T80 Neon 34mm Stainless Steel Bracelet Watch

Timex pioneered "smart watches" back in the 1980s. Now, the T80 offers relief from modern smartwatches when you feel like there is a little too much happening on your wrist. You get the time, day-date, an alarm, stop watch, and light up window. The Neon version comes in three colors but pink is definitely the color of the moment.

With almost a dozen Q models to choose from now, it has almost become a sub-brand of its own, but the original quartz diver is still the top option. Timex World Time Reissue 39mm Leather Strap Watch

Fresh from the vault, Timex went super retro with the World Time Reissue. Each city named on the bezel represents a different time zone around the world, marking 12 o'clock in that time zone. So when you set the watch to your home time, then rotate the bezel to the appropriate spot, you can can tell the time anywhere.

Timex Standard Diver 43mm Eco-Friendly Resin Strap Watch

For all your water adventures, look to the Timex Standard Diver. Black and silver contrasted dial and hands make for clear visibility, highlighted further by the red second hand and red-blue bezel. A resin strap stays light and dry without getting cold under water.

Timex Q Timex Chronograph 40mm Leather Strap Watch

Chronograph watches had a golden age in the 1970s, sported by icons like Paul Newman and Steve McQueen. This Q chronograph comes straight out of that era. From the bezel and bumpers to the sub-dials and hands, every detail is spot on. The cream and black combination gives a "panda" effect that is very sought-after in vintage chronographs.

Timex Harborside Coast 43mm Bracelet Watch

Dive watches like the Harborside demonstrate why the genre formerly reserved for the water is now perfectly at home with formalwear. That black dial and red bezel on a polished steel case and strap can fill in for any dress watch out there. That said, this is still a serious diver with a 100 m water resistance.
