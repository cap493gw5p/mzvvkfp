What are the possible causes of pain in my ankle?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Ankle pain is most often caused by sprains or acute injuries, though it can also result from less common conditions such as tendon inflammation (tendonitis), arthritis, or nerve-related issues. Common symptoms include swelling, stiffness, bruising, and difficulty bearing weight. Identifying the underlying cause is essential for proper treatment and recovery.

Explanation

Ankle pain can come from many different causes. The most common cause is a sprain, which happens when you stretch or tear the ligaments that support your ankle. Sprains often occur during activities that involve sudden stops or twists.

Other causes of ankle pain include:

Injuries: Like fractures or tendon injuries, which can happen during sports or accidents.
