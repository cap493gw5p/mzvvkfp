Breast pain, also known as mastalgia, can have different causes. It is not always linked to your menstrual cycle. Hormonal changes can still affect your body even after your period ends. Stress and anxiety might also cause breast pain.

Here are some possible reasons for breast pain after your period:

Hormonal Changes: Your body continues to experience hormonal fluctuations that could cause discomfort.

Non-cyclical Pain: Some breast pain is not linked to your menstrual cycle. It might be due to other factors like stress or injury.
