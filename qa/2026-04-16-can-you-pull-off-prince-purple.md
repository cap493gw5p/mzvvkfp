# Can You Pull Off 'Prince Purple'?

[Pull Off Prince Purple'](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/3168081544.html)

Every once in a while, a fashion trend emerges that is promptly embraced by men's style bloggers, whether it is considered a good idea or not. Earlier this year, it was the Romphim; in 2015, it was penis peepholes, a.k.a. the most effective way to get yourself arrested for lewd conduct on your morning commute. An arguably equally ill-advised (though not as sexually deviant) trend is wearing Prince purple, the latest shade to officially be named a color by the company Pantone, released in honor of the Purple One himself.

The hue commemorating Prince — who passed away in 2016 at the age of 57 from an accidental fentanyl overdose — is officially named “Love Symbol #2” after Prince’s iconic symbol (you know, that weird, froofy-looking gender sign that he was using as his name during the mid-to-late-90s). It’s the same hue as his custom-made Yamaha piano, as well as the jacket he famously wore in the 1984 film Purple Rain.

We all know that Prince could wear the shit out of a metallic lavender blazer, and other celebrities have also followed suit, including Nick Jonas and Harry Styles. And yet, the question remains: can you, the average, red-blooded male who has not had sex with Carmen Electra, pull off Prince purple? The answer: well, it all depends.

While the hue is usually associated with Batman villains or eccentric candy impresarios, the truth is that most guys can pull off purple, albeit in small doses. When used as an accent, like with a tie, socks, or a pocket square, purple can provide a much-needed splash of color to an otherwise drab ensemble. That applies doubly if you’re wearing more muted colors, like heather grey or beige, where a violet or lavender accessory can make you look effortlessly chic without coming off as a dandy.

If you’re feeling particularly bold and want to up your pastels game by throwing a purple blazer or pant into the mix, then by all means, go for it — especially during the summer, when a lavender dress shirt makes every guy look like they’re about to attend a Gatsby party. Just try to stick to more muted shades and keep the rest of your outfit fairly subdued. Otherwise, you’ll look like Grimace, and no one wants to fuck Grimace — not even the other characters in the McDonald’s universe.

TL,DR: a purple jacket is fine and purple slacks are fine, but please, for the love of God, do not ever, ever, wear both at the same time. “But what about Nick Jonas?” you might say. “He looks boss in an indigo suit. What about Robert Downey, Jr.?” My friend, you are not Nick Jonas. You are not Robert Downey, Jr.
