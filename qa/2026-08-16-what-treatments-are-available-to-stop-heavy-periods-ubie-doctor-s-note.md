Heavy menstrual bleeding, or menorrhagia, can be managed with different treatments based on individual needs and the underlying cause.

Medications:

Tranexamic Acid: This medicine helps reduce bleeding by preventing blood clots from breaking down too quickly. It is often used during the days of heavy bleeding.
