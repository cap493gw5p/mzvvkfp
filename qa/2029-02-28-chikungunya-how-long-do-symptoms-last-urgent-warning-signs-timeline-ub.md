Explanation

Chikungunya is a viral infection that often causes fever and joint pain. Most people start to feel better within 1 to 2 weeks. However, joint pain can last longer, sometimes for months or even years.

Symptoms Duration

Acute Phase: This phase usually lasts 1 to 2 weeks. During this time, people may experience fever, joint pain, headache, and rash.

Chronic Phase: Some people may have joint pain that lasts longer. This can continue for months or even years, making it hard to do daily activities.

Factors Affecting Duration

Immune Response: How your body fights the virus can affect how long symptoms last.

Age and Health: Older people or those with other health problems may have symptoms that last longer.
