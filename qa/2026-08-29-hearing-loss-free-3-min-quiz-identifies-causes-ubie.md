About the Symptom

Hearing loss describes an individual having difficulty with hearing. It may affect one or both ears and may affect only certain frequencies (pitches). This may be slowly over months to years or acutely. Any acute changes in hearing not due to ear wax should consider seeking medical attention. Hearing loss can be further broken down to trouble getting noise from the outside world to the hearing nerve (conductive) or due to the hearing nerve itself (sensorineural) or due to a mix between the two (mixed).
