Hair loss with an itchy scalp can be caused by conditions like dandruff, sensitive scalp, and certain hair care practices.

Explanation

Hair loss and an itchy scalp can occur together for several reasons. One common cause is dandruff, which can make your scalp itchy and lead to hair loss if not treated. A sensitive scalp is another reason. This means your scalp can get irritated easily, which could make it itchy and cause hair to fall out.

Sensitive Scalp: Some people have scalps that are more sensitive to substances like hair products or weather changes, which can cause itchiness and hair loss.

Hair Care Practices: How you take care of your hair can also affect your scalp. Using too many products or washing your hair too much can make your scalp itchy and cause hair loss.

Taking care of your scalp and hair gently can help prevent these issues. If you have concerns, talking to a healthcare provider might be helpful.
