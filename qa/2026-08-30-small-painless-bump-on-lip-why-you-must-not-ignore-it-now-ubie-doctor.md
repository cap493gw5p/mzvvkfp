Explanation

When you notice a small, pain-free bump on your lip, it may be a benign (non-cancerous) lesion. There are a few possibilities for what it could be. One type is a benign vascular lesion, which is a growth made from blood vessels. These often appear on the lips and do not hurt. Another possibility is a benign fibrous histiocytoma. This is a growth made from fibrous tissues and can sometimes be found on the lower lip. A rarer chance is that it might be a schwannoma, which is a benign tumor that develops from the cells around nerves. Even though these conditions are generally not serious, the only way to be sure of what the bump is involves an evaluation by a doctor. They might suggest further tests or a biopsy if needed.
