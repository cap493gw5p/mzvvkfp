What causes pain in my lower back when standing for too long?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Lower back pain when standing too long is often caused by muscle fatigue, poor posture, or added strain on the spine as your body works to stay upright. Prolonged standing can compress the lumbar discs, tighten the hip flexors, and overwork the postural muscles, leading to aching or stiffness. In some cases, underlying conditions like spinal stenosis, herniated discs, or sciatica may also contribute.

Because lower back pain can stem from many possible causes—ranging from simple muscle strain to more serious spinal issues—it's important to understand what's driving your symptoms before deciding on next steps.
