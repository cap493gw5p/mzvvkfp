Cloudy urine in males may be a sign that there is pus in the urethra. This happens when there is an infection or inflammation in the urinary tract. Urinalysis, a test of urine, can help find out if there is pus. This test looks at the urine to see if there are any signs of infection or other problems.

Possible Causes:

Infections: These can be caused by bacteria or other germs. Infections can make the urine look cloudy.

Urethral Trichomoniasis: This is a type of infection caused by a tiny parasite. It can affect the urethra and make the urine cloudy.

Certain foods and substances can also cause cloudy urine. Other conditions, such as dehydration, can also make your urine look cloudy.

If a person notices cloudy urine, it is a good idea to talk to a healthcare provider. They can help figure out what might be causing it and suggest what to do next.
