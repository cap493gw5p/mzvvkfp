Rapid mood swings are a hallmark of rapid-cycling bipolar disorder, a condition in which individuals experience four or more distinct mood episodes—alternating between depression, mania, or hypomania—within a 12-month period. These shifts can occur over days, weeks, or even within a single day, disrupting relationships, work, and daily functioning. Common triggers include stress, poor sleep, hormonal changes, and certain medications.

Because rapid mood changes can also point to other conditions—like borderline personality disorder, ADHD, or thyroid issues—getting clarity early matters. Explanation

Rapid-cycling bipolar disorder is a condition where individuals experience frequent mood changes. These mood changes happen more than four times a year, switching between depression and mania or hypomania.

Key Features:

Depression: Feeling very sad or down.

Mania or Hypomania: Feeling very happy, energetic, or irritable.

People with rapid-cycling bipolar disorder can have a hard time managing these mood swings. It is important to seek help if you or someone you know is experiencing these symptoms.
