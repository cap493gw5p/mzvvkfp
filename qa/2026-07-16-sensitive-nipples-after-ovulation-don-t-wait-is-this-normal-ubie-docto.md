Yes, it is common to experience sensitive or sore nipples after ovulation. This tenderness is caused by a rise in progesterone and estrogen during the luteal phase (the two weeks between ovulation and your next period), which increases blood flow and fluid retention in breast tissue.

Common causes of post-ovulation nipple sensitivity include:

Hormonal shifts (progesterone surge)

Premenstrual syndrome (PMS)

Early pregnancy

Birth control changes

While mild soreness is typically normal, persistent, severe, or one-sided nipple pain can sometimes signal an underlying issue worth investigating.
