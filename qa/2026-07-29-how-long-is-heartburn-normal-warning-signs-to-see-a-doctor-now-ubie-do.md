Internal Medicine

Heartburn is usually harmless, but you should be concerned if it happens more than twice a week, doesn't improve with over-the-counter antacids, or is accompanied by difficulty swallowing, chest pain, unexplained weight loss, or vomiting. Frequent or persistent heartburn can signal gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), which may lead to complications like esophagitis or Barrett's esophagus if left untreated.

Because heartburn symptoms can overlap with more serious conditions—including heart-related issues—it's important to identify the underlying cause quickly.
