Lower back pain from sitting is often caused by poor posture and inadequate chair support. To relieve it, use an ergonomic chair with proper lumbar support, keep your feet flat on the floor, and maintain a neutral spine position with your hips and knees at roughly 90 degrees. Take short movement breaks every 30 minutes to reduce muscle stiffness and improve circulation.

Because lower back pain can stem from many causes—ranging from simple posture issues to underlying conditions that need attention—it's smart to understand what may actually be driving your discomfort before assuming it's just your chair. Explanation

Lower back pain from sitting can be uncomfortable, but there are ways to help. One important thing is to use a chair that supports your lower back. This can help reduce strain on your spine. Additionally, sitting in a neutral position, where your back is straight and your feet are flat on the floor, can make a difference.

Here are some tips to consider:

Adjust Your Chair: Make sure your chair supports your lower back. Look for chairs with lumbar support features.

Neutral Sitting Position: Try to sit with your back straight and feet flat on the ground. This can help reduce the effort needed to maintain a good posture.
