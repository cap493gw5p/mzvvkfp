# Which 'Bachelor' and 'Bachelorette' Couples Actually Stayed Together?

It's pretty well known that even after all the dates, first kisses, and roses given out, the lucky couple left standing at the end of The Bachelor, The Bachelorette, or Bachelor in Paradise may not stay together. In fact, some might say that the stats for couples that actually get married after meeting on the long-running ABC show are...not promising. But if you're the type that likes to look on the brighter side of things, check out this list of all the Bachelor Nation couples that are still together.

The Bachelor

Jason Mesnick and Molly Malaney

Jason and Molly's path to love wasn't so straightforward. On his season of The Bachelor, Jason initially proposed to Melissa Rycroft, but by the time the finale rolled around, he broke off the engagement to pursue a relationship with Molly. They later got married and have two children.

Sean Lowe and Catherine Giudici

About a year after Sean's season of The Bachelor aired, the couple got married, and they now have two kids and are expecting another. As Insider pointed out, this makes Sean the only Bachelor that has stayed married to the winner of his season, as the two other Bachelors that are married returned to their runner-ups.

Arie Luyendyk Jr. and Lauren Burnham

While they're happy now, Arie and Lauren had a crazy ride to the altar. He first proposed to Becca Kufrin on his Bachelor season, but then broke it off with her a few weeks later after he realized he still had feelings for Lauren. Arie and Lauren got married in January of 2019, and they welcomed a daughter in May 2019.

The Bachelorette

Trista Rehn and Ryan Sutter

The couple, now parents to two kids, met on the very first season of The Bachelorette in 2003, and they've been together ever since, making them Bachelor Nation's longest-running couple. (They're routinely trotted out every few years to remind doubting viewers that the show does, in fact, work sometimes.) They've also made appearances on TV shows like Marriage Boot Camp, Who Wants to Be a Millionaire, and a couple of episodes of The Bachelor and Bachelorette.

Ashley Hebert and J.P. Rosenbaum

Ashley and J.P. got married in 2012 after her season of The Bachelorette, and they share two kids together. Their wedding was aired in an ABC special that was officiated by Bachelor and Bachelorette host Chris Harrison himself.

Desiree Hartsock and Chris Siegfried

There was some controversy during Desiree's season of The Bachelorette, as the frontrunner, Brooks Forester, broke up with her in the second-to-last week of the show. She went on to pick Chris, and the couple got married in 2015. The parents-of-two also appeared on Marriage Boot Camp in 2018.

Joelle "JoJo" Fletcher and Jordan Rodgers

The couple met and got engaged in 2016, but they say they're in no rush to tie the knot. They're busy with their web series "Engaged with JoJo and Jordan," and they're due to start filming their real estate series Crash Pad, which will air on CNBC's Real Estate Thursdays lineup. Jordan recently "re-proposed" to JoJo, saying that they wanted a chance to experience their engagement without having cameras around.

Rachel Lindsay and Bryan Abasolo

After a very dramatic episode in which frontrunner Peter Kraus told Rachel that he would prefer to be in a relationship and not propose right away, Rachel chose Bryan at the conclusion to her season in 2017. The couple got married in August 2019.

Bachelor Pad

Holly Durst and Blake Julian

Holly appeared on season 12 of The Bachelor, and Blake was from season 7 of The Bachelorette, and they met and got engaged during the second season of the short-lived Bachelor spinoff Bachelor Pad in 2011. They got married a year later, and while Bachelor Pad was canceled in 2012, it was later replaced by Bachelor in Paradise.

Bachelor in Paradise

Jade Roper and Tanner Tolbert

Jade and Tanner were the first Bachelor in Paradise couple to get married, and their wedding aired on ABC in 2016. The couple has a daughter and a son together, and following the path of many Bachelor Nation couples, Jade and Tanner later appeared on Marriage Boot Camp.

Carly Waddell and Evan Bass

The couple had a truly Bachelor Nation-filled wedding when they got married in Mexico in 2017 after meeting on the third season of Bachelor in Paradise. Other Bachelor alums like Jade Roper and Tanner Tolbert attended the nuptials, and Chris Harrison officiated the ceremony. The couple has a daughter together, and they're expecting another child soon. Evan also has three kids from a prior marriage.

Raven Gates and Adam Gottschalk

Raven and Adam have been together for almost two years, but they're one of the more low-key Bachelor couples. They didn't get engaged after meeting on season four of Paradise, instead choosing to focus on building a strong relationship first before getting engaged in July 2019. And when it comes time for their 2020 wedding, the couple told The Toronto Star that they don't want a televised ceremony.

Astrid Loch and Kevin Wendt

Another couple that broke up during their season then got back together, Astrid and Kevin have been going strong since season five of Paradise. Astrid moved to Toronto to be with Kevin, and they recently got engaged.

Ashley Iaconetti and Jared Haibon

The couple met during Bachelor in Paradise's second season, but they didn't begin dating until 2018. Jared previously dated The Bachelor's Caila Quinn and Ashley had been in a relationship with her Bachelor Winter Games castmate Kevin Wendt. Ashley and Jared got married in August 2019.

Dylan Barbour and Hannah Godwin

Dylan and Hannah spent most of their season on Paradise declaring their love for each other and making out, and currently engaged couple are currently planning their wedding.

Dean Ulgert and Caelynn Miller-Keyes

Dean and Caelynn met on season six of Bachelor in Paradise, and they left the show together later in the season. They've been together ever since, and the couple frequently showcases their travels on Instagram.
