Explanation

When your period is late by about a week, it might be because of early pregnancy even if you do not have any other symptoms. In early pregnancy, some women do not notice any symptoms commonly associated with early pregnancy like nausea or breast tenderness. Each person may have a different experience of pregnancy. Pregnancy tests may also show a negative result if taken too early, even though you are pregnant. In pregnancy, as time passes, hormone levels rise, and symptoms may develop. It is important to remember that there are other reasons for a missed period, such as stress or issues with hormone balance. If you suspect that you might be pregnant, you might wait a few more days and take another test or talk with a healthcare provider.
