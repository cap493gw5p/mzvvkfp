Can pus from the urethra indicate a serious health issue?

Pus coming from the urethra is not normal and can indicate a serious health issue. It can be an early sign of a urinary tract problem. This is because the presence of pus suggests that there might be an infection or inflammation happening in the urinary tract.

Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs): Infections in the urinary tract can cause pus to appear in the urethra. This is often due to bacteria entering the urinary system and causing inflammation.

Prostatic Issues: Another possible cause of pus from the urethra is a prostatic abscess. This is when pus collects in the prostate gland, which can lead to discharge from the urethra.
